What's built

Twenty agents. One pool. A cap that holds.

The pool pays the vendor. An agent holds none of the funds. A spend is allowed only after the contract reserves it.

The pool

1

One contract, Rova. The cap is fixed at construction. reserve locks funds. commit pays the vendor and leaves the unused amount in the pool.

The token

rUSD

A public-mint ERC-20 with 18 decimals. The demo funds 30 against a cap of 10, so a broken spend has tokens past the policy. The token has no value.

Check, then pay

20

Twenty agents each spend 1 after reading the token balance. All twenty pay. The cap was 10.

Reserve, then pay

10

The same twenty agents lock 1 first. Ten fit. Ten are refused. The vendor is paid 10.
